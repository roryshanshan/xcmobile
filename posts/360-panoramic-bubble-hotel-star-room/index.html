360° Panoramic Bubble Hotel Star Room – Transparent Dome for Glamping & Resorts

Modular Transparent Structure · UV-Proof Polycarbonate · Easy to Install · Factory Direct Supply

Our 360° panoramic bubble hotel star room is a transparent modular dome structure designed for eco-resorts, luxury glamping, boutique homestays, and event use. Constructed with aviation-grade aluminum and UV-resistant polycarbonate, this dome provides an immersive stargazing experience while offering safety, comfort, and portability.

📐 Technical Specifications of the Panoramic Bubble Hotel Dome

Feature Description
Materials Transparent polycarbonate shell + aviation-grade aluminum frame
Available Sizes 2m / 3m / 4m / 5m / 9m diameter bubble domes
Structure Type Frameless modular dome, no internal metal skeleton
Assembly System Modular splicing for fast shipping and on-site installation
Installation Time 2–4 hours with 2–3 workers
Light Transmission UV-resistant high-clarity polycarbonate for natural lighting & stargazing
Wind & Weather Resistance Withstands up to Level 8 winds; waterproof sealing around base joints
Temperature Resistance Designed for -40°C to +60°C outdoor conditions
Portability Reusable structure, relocatable and ideal for mobile glamping setups

🌌 Key Applications of the Transparent Bubble Hotel Dome

  • 🛌 Glamping accommodation domes for resorts, eco-parks, and boutique hotels
  • 🔭 Stargazing rooms with full 360° sky visibility, perfect for couples or astronomy lovers
  • 🌿 Eco-lodging cabins designed for minimal environmental impact
  • 🎪 Modular event domes for exhibitions, showrooms, or pop-up shops
  • ☀️ VIP lounges or relaxation pods with natural lighting and full privacy

❓ Frequently Asked Questions about the Bubble Hotel Star Room

Q: Is the bubble dome weatherproof and wind-resistant?
A: Yes, the structure withstands wind levels up to 8 and features waterproof sealing for long-term outdoor use.

Q: Can the transparent dome hotel be customized?
A: Absolutely. Available sizes range from 2m to 9m. You can choose partial tinting, privacy curtains, or integrated ventilation.

Q: How difficult is installation?
A: It takes only 2–4 hours with 2–3 workers, and no heavy machinery is needed.

Q: Is it reusable or portable?
A: Yes! The modular design allows for easy disassembly and relocation for repeated outdoor or event use.
