## Townhouse Built with Detachable Container Houses

Our townhouse is designed using detachable container houses, which can be easily assembled and disassembled. This makes them a highly flexible and convenient housing solution for various uses.

The modular design allows easy customization and expansion according to your needs. The townhouse features a modern, stylish exterior with large windows that bring in abundant natural light and ensure good ventilation. Inside, it offers a spacious, comfortable layout equipped with all essential amenities for everyday living.

This innovative building approach is ideal for residential projects, temporary housing, offices, glamping sites, and more. It combines quick installation with cost-effective transport and reusability.
