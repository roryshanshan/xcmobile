Our Detachable Container House redefines flexible living with its innovative modular design, enabling easy disassembly and relocation without structural compromise.

Featuring bolt-on wall panels and a reinforced steel frame, it assembles in under 6 hours and adapts to residential, commercial, or emergency use.

Built with galvanized steel (ASTM A653) and fire-retardant insulated panels, it withstands harsh climates (-30°C to 50°C) while maintaining energy efficiency (U-value ≤0.35 W/m²K).

Customize layouts, sizes (20-40㎡), and add-ons like solar roofs or smart HVAC systems for off-grid resilience.

Ideal for remote worksites, disaster relief, eco-tourism, or urban expansions, its 95% recyclable materials align with global sustainability goals.

Our Advantage

Modular & Rapid Assembly

  1. Precision Prefabrication: All structural components (walls, roof, flooring) are laser-cut and robot-welded off-site with ±0.3mm tolerance, eliminating on-site measurement errors.

  2. Plug-and-Play System: Bolt-together connections require only 3 workers and basic tools (wrench, crane), enabling full assembly of a 40ft unit in 8 hours – 90% faster than brick-and-mortar methods.

Cost-Efficient & Reusable

  1. Upfront Savings: Standardized production cuts material waste by 22% and labor costs by 60% vs. conventional builds.

  2. Circular Economy Design: Hot-dip galvanized steel frame (350g/m² zinc coating) ensures 12-year rust-free performance, reusable across 5+ project cycles.

  3. Tax Incentives: Qualifies for green building subsidies in EU/US (LEED/BREEAM credits) and 30% faster depreciation vs. permanent structures.

Durable & Weather-Resistant

  1. Structural Integrity: Q345B carbon steel columns (8mm thickness) support 25-ton roof loads – withstand 1.5m snow accumulation.

  2. Climate Defense:

Arctic Proof: Triple-glazed windows + 120mm PU insulation maintains +20°C indoor temps at -40°C.

Tropical Resilience: Corrugated roof sheds 300mm/hr rainfall; zinc-aluminium alloy walls resist 95% humidity corrosion.

  1. Wind Rating: Aerodynamic design passes 150 km/h wind tunnel testing (equivalent to Category 4 hurricane).

Eco-Friendly Design

  1. Material Transparency:

95% Recycled Steel: Sourced from certified mills (ISO 14064 carbon-tracked).

Zero VOC： Fire-resistant Rockwool panels (100% basalt fibre) + formaldehyde-free plywood.

  1. Energy Autonomy:

Pre-installed Conduits: Roof supports 5kW solar arrays (4hr sunlight = 24hr power for LED/AC).

Heat Recovery Ventilation: Recaptures 85% of HVAC energy via ERV systems.

Customizable Configurations

  1. Flexible Footprints:

Compact Units: 6㎡ standalone studios → 1,200㎡ complexes (4 floors, 200+ occupants).

Hybrid Systems: Combine with glass facades/steel decks for offices, schools, or clinics.

  1. Tech Integration:

Smart Ready: KNX/Ethernet pre-wiring + wall-mounted control panels.

IoT Optional: Remote monitoring of temp/humidity/security via 4G/WiFi.
