<section class="xc-section-shell">
  <h2>Project orders usually focus on these checkpoints</h2>
  <div class="xc-feature-grid">
    <div class="xc-feature-card">
      <span>Before production</span>
      <strong>Are drawings, layout direction, and option boundaries clearly confirmed</strong>
      <p>The earlier those decisions are aligned, the less risk there is of rework and repeated clarification later.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="xc-feature-card">
      <span>During production</span>
      <strong>Are progress, changes, and material scope communicated clearly</strong>
      <p>Project communication matters more than image presentation, especially when quantities increase.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="xc-feature-card">
      <span>Before shipment</span>
      <strong>Are packing, loading, documents, and inspection expectations aligned</strong>
      <p>These factors directly influence transport timing and site handover efficiency at destination.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="xc-section-shell">
  <h2>What we usually recommend confirming together</h2>
  <div class="xc-spec-grid">
    <div class="xc-spec-card">
      <span>Standard scope</span>
      <strong>Which systems are included by default</strong>
      <p>This gives buyers a clearer way to compare the completion level of different product families.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="xc-spec-card">
      <span>Upgrade range</span>
      <strong>Which materials and features can be specified upward</strong>
      <p>Important for premium projects and for matching different target-market positioning.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="xc-spec-card">
      <span>Packing method</span>
      <strong>Fully assembled, flat-pack, or modular split shipment</strong>
      <p>This directly affects transport planning and site sequencing.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="xc-spec-card">
      <span>Technical files</span>
      <strong>Drawings, layouts, and supporting references</strong>
      <p>Useful for internal review and for moving the project forward with more confidence.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
