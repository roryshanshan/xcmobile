Custom Office Apple Pod | Prefab Mobile Cabin Manufacturer in China

Monday, Jun 2, 2025 | 2 minute read | Updated at Monday, Jun 2, 2025

@ Gavin

🌟 Product Overview

The Office Apple Pod is a mobile, prefabricated unit with minimalist styling and high functionality. Designed for fast deployment and long-lasting performance, it’s suitable for use as:

  • Remote office or studio
  • Tiny home / Airbnb cabin
  • B&B unit or outdoor guest room
  • Commercial space or sales booth

Constructed with a hot-dip galvanized steel frame, SPC waterproof flooring, and carbon fiber composite walls, this pod delivers comfort, strength, and thermal performance in any climate.

📐 Specifications

Item Details
Available Sizes 20FT: 5.8×2.2×2.45m / 40FT: 11.6×2.2×2.45m
Structure Hot-dip galvanized steel, one-piece molded walls
Wall Material Carbon fiber composite, thermal insulation 0.24W
Glass Double-glazed bridge-cut aluminum curtain walls
Interior Bedroom, Bathroom, Kitchen, Desk (Customizable)

Temperature Range -30℃ to +50℃
Point Load Capacity 2500 kg

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is an Office Apple Pod?
A: It’s a stylish mobile pod that looks like an Apple phone, originally developed by Shanghai Kuanrui in 2019. It’s designed for both personal and commercial use, now sold in 20+ countries.

Q: Are materials recyclable?
A: Yes, all structural and finish materials are recyclable and environmentally friendly.

Q: What’s the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
A: Just 1 unit! Orders of full containers get free customization options.

Q: How long is the warranty?
A: 5 years for the steel frame; 1 year for interior finishes.
