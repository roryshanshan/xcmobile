# Space Capsule vs Apple Cabin: Which One Fits Your Project?

Space capsules and apple cabins are both attractive options for tourism and accommodation projects, but they usually serve slightly different positioning goals.

## Choose Space Capsule When

- You want a stronger futuristic image
- The room itself should become a marketing highlight
- The project needs a premium, highly visual accommodation unit
- You want to emphasize panoramic experience and product identity

Main page: [Space Capsule]({{< relref "/space-capsule.md" >}})

## Choose Apple Cabin When

- You want a distinctive but more flexible cabin concept
- The project needs a recognizable silhouette without looking too futuristic
- You want to balance appearance with broader use scenarios
- You may need more concept variation across one product family

Main page: [Apple Cabin]({{< relref "/apple-cabin.md" >}})

## Compare by Business Goal

### For Premium Destination Marketing

Space capsule often works better when the accommodation unit is the central visual attraction.

### For Branded Tourism Cabin Projects

Apple cabin often works better when the project needs a memorable cabin identity with room to adapt the concept.

### For Mixed Product Strategy

Some operators may combine both:

- Space capsule for flagship rooms
- Apple cabin for broader room inventory

## The Better Question to Ask

Instead of asking only “Which one is better?”, ask:

- What kind of guests are we targeting?
- What room type will be used in the marketing materials?
- Which unit best supports the project’s price positioning?

Once that is clear, the product choice becomes much easier.
