## Why Buyers Move Up to E7

- The larger footprint supports higher-value room planning.
- It is better suited to suite-style hospitality concepts.
- It gives resort buyers more flexibility for family or premium-stay positioning.
- It helps projects that need a more spacious room type without leaving the capsule product family.

## Configuration Highlights

- Integrated galvanized steel structure
- Aviation aluminum outer shell with fluorocarbon finish
- Low-E insulating glass system
- Polyurethane insulation system
- Smart lock and integrated lighting logic
- Bathroom and utility systems for hospitality use
- Larger suite-style room planning than E5

## Best-Fit Projects

- premium resort suites
- scenic-area family rooms
- boutique hospitality clusters
- projects that need a larger room type than E5 or E6

## Model Comparison

- Choose [E5](/posts/e5-modelspace-capsule-details/) when compact premium rooms are enough and balcony appeal matters.
- Choose [E6](/posts/e6-modelspace-capsule-details/) when you want more indoor living function at compact length.
- Choose `E7` when room size and suite-style positioning are the top priority.

## Planning Note for Export Buyers

<div class="xc-highlight-box">
  <p>The E7 is a larger unit than E5 or E6, so transport route, unloading conditions, and site access should be reviewed early in the inquiry stage.</p>
