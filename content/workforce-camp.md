## Typical Use Cases

- Labor accommodation
- Site office and management office
- Temporary camp deployment
- Remote project housing
- Support buildings such as dining, administration, and utility space

## What Matters Most in These Projects

- Packing efficiency and transport planning
- Repeated deployment and easy assembly
- Standardized rooms and repeatable specifications
- Fast quotation and practical options rather than excessive customization
- Clear communication about utility connections and site preparation

## Best Inquiry Format

- Destination country and nearest port
- Required quantity and preferred delivery window
- Number of people to accommodate
- Need for dormitory, office, bathroom, kitchen, or workshop functions
- Whether the project is temporary, semi-permanent, or long-term
