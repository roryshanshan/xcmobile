  • The product itself helps market the destination, not only provide a room.

Product and Shipping Notes

Important: space capsule units are not standard ISO shipping containers. Shipment planning should begin with the destination country, nearest port, site access, and chosen model.

This is why we discuss model, quantity, and destination together before confirming the export approach.

Typical Use Cases

  • Scenic-area room clusters
  • Glamping and eco-tourism accommodation
  • Resort signature rooms
  • Boutique hospitality projects
  • Demonstration units for dealers or destination developers

What Buyers Usually Want to Confirm

  • Which model best fits the target room count and project positioning
  • Whether the project needs a more compact unit or a larger suite
  • What interior options should be standardized before production
  • How transport and unloading will work for the destination
  • Whether local installation resources are available onsite
