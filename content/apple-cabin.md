## Why Buyers Choose Apple Cabin

- The profile is easier to brand into a tourism destination concept.
- It works well in scenic projects where exterior identity matters.
- Buyers can discuss multiple variants without leaving the same product family.
- It fits both accommodation and selected commercial or office-style uses.

## Core Product Reference

| Item | Reference information |
| --- | --- |
| Typical reference size | 11.5 m × 3.3 m × 3.2 m |
| Approximate weight | 9 tons |
| Main frame | Galvanized steel frame |
| Outer shell | Aluminum alloy shell |
| Insulation and glazing | Hollow Low-E tempered glass curtain wall system |
| Climate note | Previously listed for high wind resistance and low-temperature environments |
| Typical applications | Tourism, scenic spots, destination accommodation, branded cabins |

## What This Product Line Is Good For

- Boutique scenic accommodation
- Destination room clusters
- Signature cabins for tourism parks
- Eye-catching modular rooms for operators who care about visual marketing
- Selected office or specialty pod adaptations
