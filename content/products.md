<section class="xc-section-shell">
  <h2>How serious buyers usually choose faster</h2>
  <div class="xc-feature-grid">
    <div class="xc-feature-card">
      <span>Start with the project mood</span>
      <strong>Should the unit feel iconic, natural, or purely practical</strong>
      <p>This usually determines whether Space Capsule, Apple Cabin, Wooden Cabin, or Container Solutions should be reviewed first.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="xc-feature-card">
      <span>Then check execution limits</span>
      <strong>Transport, lifting, container loading, and site access</strong>
      <p>Different product lines are not equally sensitive to shipping size, handling method, and installation conditions.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="xc-feature-card">
      <span>Finally prepare decision material</span>
      <strong>Quantity, destination, target user, and reference pages</strong>
      <p>The more complete that first package is, the easier it is to avoid low-value quoting and repeated back-and-forth.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
