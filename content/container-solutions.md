## Recommended Buyers

- Camp and workforce accommodation developers
- Contractors handling temporary site offices
- Importers looking for modular inventory with broader market demand
- Buyers who need simpler transportation and more standardized production

## What to Confirm Before Quotation

- Destination country and nearest discharge port
- Required quantity and delivery schedule
- Whether the project needs bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, or office fit-out
- Installation conditions and site access
- Whether the units are for rental, self-use, project delivery, or distribution

For resort-driven projects with stronger architectural emphasis, start from [Space Capsule]({{< relref "/space-capsule.md" >}}) or [Apple Cabin]({{< relref "/apple-cabin.md" >}}) instead.
