## Why Buyers Choose the E5

- It gives the project a premium room identity in a more compact footprint than E7.
- The balcony-style experience helps position the unit as a scenic accommodation product.
- It is a strong fit for operators who want flagship units for phase one of a hospitality project.
- It works well when the room itself is part of the marketing story.

## Configuration Highlights

- Integrated galvanized steel structure
- 2.0 mm aviation aluminum outer shell
- Low-E insulating glass system
- Panoramic balcony concept
- Smart lock and integrated lighting logic
- Heating and cooling air-conditioning support
- Full bathroom integration for resort-style room use

## Best-Fit Projects

- Scenic-area room clusters
- Glamping and eco-tourism accommodation
- Boutique resort rooms
- Destination projects that need a premium showcase unit
