## What Makes E6 Different

- E5 emphasizes balcony-style experience and a lower entry price.
- E6 emphasizes a living-room arrangement and a more residential feel.
- In the original layout comparison, the bathroom position shifts from the side in E5 to the middle in E6.

## Best-Fit Buyer Type

The E6 is usually a better fit when the project wants:

- more indoor usability at compact length
- a more residential room story
- a room that can feel less like a pure scenic pod and more like a small suite

## Which Projects Usually Prefer E6

- resort rooms that need more indoor function
- premium cabin concepts that want a compact suite feeling
- buyers comparing layout efficiency before moving up to E7

## If You Are Comparing E5, E6, and E7

- Choose [E5](/posts/e5-modelspace-capsule-details/) for balcony-oriented compact premium rooms.
- Choose `E6` for the compact model with more interior living function.
- Choose [E7](/posts/e7-modelspace-capsule-details/) when the project needs a larger suite-scale room.
