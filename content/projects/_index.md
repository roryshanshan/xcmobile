  <h2>What Serious Buyers Usually Confirm from a Project Page</h2>
  <div class="xc-spec-grid">
    <div class="xc-spec-card">
      <span>Location</span>
      <strong>Which country or region the project is in</strong>
      <p>This helps frame transport difficulty, climate expectations, and buyer context.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="xc-spec-card">
      <span>Project Type</span>
      <strong>Hospitality, workforce housing, or support use</strong>
      <p>The same product can behave very differently depending on the business model behind it.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="xc-spec-card">
      <span>Deployment Scale</span>
      <strong>Whether it is a pilot unit or a batch project</strong>
      <p>Scale affects pricing discussion, shipment planning, and delivery rhythm.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="xc-spec-card">
      <span>Execution Logic</span>
      <strong>Shipping, installation, and site conditions</strong>
      <p>Without that layer, project references are much less useful for real decision-making.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
