# Prefab Solutions for Scenic Areas and Boutique Hotels

This page is for projects that need more than simple accommodation.  
The goal here is to create a stay experience that feels premium, memorable, and aligned with the destination.

## Recommended Product Direction

- [Space Capsule]({{< relref "/space-capsule.md" >}}) for high-visual-impact hospitality rooms
- [Apple Cabin]({{< relref "/apple-cabin.md" >}}) for branded cabin concepts
- [Wooden Cabin](/wooden-cabin/) for softer architectural language in natural settings
- [Villa]({{< relref "/posts/villa.md" >}}) for larger or more residential-style layouts

## What This Buyer Type Usually Needs

- Premium look and stronger design language
- Good glazing, light, and view orientation
- Room plans suitable for higher-value guests
- Clear understanding of transport and site assembly limitations
- A supplier that can discuss customization early in the process

## Content Priorities for This Audience

- Project photos and layout references
- Materials and finish explanations
- Delivery and installation planning
- Honest explanation of what can be customized and what should stay standardized
- Clear contact path for concept-stage discussions
