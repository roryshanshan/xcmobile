# Prefab Units for Resorts and Glamping Projects

This solution is designed for buyers who want accommodation units that are faster to deploy than conventional construction while still creating a strong guest experience.

## Best-Fit Product Families

- [Space Capsule]({{< relref "/space-capsule.md" >}}) for premium and distinctive resort rooms
- [Apple Cabin]({{< relref "/apple-cabin.md" >}}) for destination projects with strong visual branding
- [Wooden Cabin](/wooden-cabin/) for eco-lodging, retreat, and nature-oriented concepts
- [360 Panoramic Bubble Hotel Star Room]({{< relref "/posts/360 Panoramic Bubble Hotel Star Room.md" >}}) for niche scenic concepts and stargazing experiences

## What Resort Buyers Usually Care About

- Exterior appearance and how the unit photographs for online marketing
- Guest comfort, insulation, glazing, and bathroom quality
- Site conditions and how fast the project can begin operating
- Quantity planning for room clusters, circulation, and public areas
- Utility connection and maintenance expectations

## Suggested Inquiry Information

- Destination country and climate conditions
- Number of guest rooms planned in phase one
- Preferred room size and target nightly rate positioning
- Need for bathroom, kitchenette, deck, or panoramic glazing
- Whether the site is flat, sloped, coastal, mountain, or remote
